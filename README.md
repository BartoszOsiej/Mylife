## What this is

*Shadows of True Living* is the opening volume of **LIFE** — a long-form literary
work exploring memory, identity and the quiet weight of everyday choices. It is
published as a free-to-read web edition.

## The author

I am a systems developer by day (Rust, eBPF, compilers — see
[@BartoszOsiej](https://github.com/BartoszOsiej)) and a writer by conviction.
This book lives on the same account as my kernels and compilers on purpose:
both are long, patient constructions.
